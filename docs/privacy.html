Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SuMeeting (the macOS application and the companion Chrome extension, together the “App”) handles your information. The App is developed and operated by Valentyn Bratkevych (“we”, “us”). Contact: bratkevychv@gmail.com.

SuMeeting is designed as a local-first tool: your recordings, transcripts and summaries are processed and kept on your own Mac and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive. We do not operate a server that receives, stores or analyses your meetings, and we do not have access to your Google account data.

1. Information the App handles

1.1 Meeting recordings

When you start a recording in Google Meet, the Chrome extension captures the audio of the call (other participants) and your microphone, and the video of the call. The recording is sent only to the SuMeeting app running on your Mac (over localhost). You decide when recording starts and stops.

1.2 Meeting metadata from Google Meet

The extension reads the meeting title, the Meet room code, the names of participants shown in the call and which participant is speaking, from the Google Meet page. This is used to label speakers and to name the meeting.

1.3 Transcripts, summaries and action points

Transcripts are produced from the recording; summaries and action points are produced from the transcript (see section 3). They are stored in a local database on your Mac.

1.4 Google user data

The App accesses the following Google user data with your explicit consent (OAuth 2.0):

DataScopePurpose
Your Google account email address drive.file (read from the Drive about endpoint) To identify the signed-in account inside the App and confirm it is eligible to use the App.
Files in your Google Drive that SuMeeting itself created https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file To store your meeting recordings in a SuMeeting folder in your Drive (when you select Drive storage), to play them back, and to temporarily hold a recording in SuMeeting/Transit while the cloud transcription engine processes it. The App cannot see, list or modify any other file in your Drive.
Calendar events on your primary calendar https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.readonly To find the event that matches a recorded Google Meet call (by Meet room code and start time, within a window of a few hours around the recording) and read its title, time, Meet link and attendee list (display name, email address, response status, organizer), so that the saved meeting has an accurate participant list. The App never creates, modifies or deletes events.

OAuth tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac. The Google sign-in is optional for recording and local transcription, but required for Drive storage, the cloud transcription engine and Calendar-based participants.

2. How the App uses Google user data

  • Drive files are used only to store and play back your own recordings and to transfer a recording to the transcription worker (section 3.2). Transit files are deleted immediately after transcription completes; if the App is interrupted, leftover transit files are deleted automatically on the next start.
  • Calendar data is used only to derive the participant list of a recorded meeting. From the matched event the App keeps the participants’ names (and email addresses, from which a display name is derived when none is set) and the event identifier, together with the meeting in the local database. Other event content is not retained.
  • Email address is used only for display and eligibility checks inside the App.

Google user data is not used for advertising, is not sold, is not used to train machine-learning or AI models, and is not read by humans except you, unless required by law or with your explicit consent for support purposes.

3. Third-party services (data sharing)

The App shares data with third parties only as needed to provide features you enable:

3.1 Anthropic — Claude Code (summaries and action points)

If you set up Claude Code, the App sends the meeting transcript together with meeting metadata (title, date, and the participant names — which may come from your Google Calendar event) to Anthropic’s Claude through the Claude Code tool, under your own Anthropic account, to generate the summary and action points. This processing is governed by Anthropic’s terms and privacy policy. You can skip Claude Code setup; the App works without summaries.

3.2 RunPod — cloud transcription engine (optional)

If you select the “Cloud GPU” transcription engine, the recording is transcribed on a GPU worker operated by us on RunPod infrastructure in EU data centres. To transfer the recording, the App uploads it to SuMeeting/Transit in your Google Drive and passes the worker a short-lived (≤ 1 hour) Drive access token limited to files created by the App. The worker downloads the file, transcribes it in memory and returns the transcript to the App on your Mac; the worker does not keep the recording, the token or the transcript after the job completes, and the transit file is deleted from your Drive by the App. If you select the “Local” engine, nothing leaves your Mac.

3.3 Google

Drive and Calendar data are exchanged directly between your Mac and Google’s APIs, subject to Google’s privacy policy.

No other third parties receive your data. We do not use analytics or tracking services.

4. Storage, retention and deletion

  • Transcripts, summaries, participant lists and metadata are stored in a local SQLite database in ~/Library/Application Support/SuMeeting on your Mac.
  • Recordings are stored either in the same folder on your Mac or in the SuMeeting folder of your Google Drive, according to your setting.
  • You can configure automatic deletion of recordings by age and by total disk usage in Settings → Storage, and delete any meeting at any time from the App.
  • Signing out of Google in the App deletes the stored tokens from the Keychain. You can also revoke the App’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the App will stop accessing your Google data immediately.
  • Deleting the App and its Application Support folder removes all locally stored data.

5. Security

OAuth tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain. Communication with Google, Anthropic and RunPod uses HTTPS. The transcription worker receives only a short-lived, narrowly scoped token. The App is signed and notarized by Apple and updates are cryptographically signed.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy — Limited Use

SuMeeting’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
