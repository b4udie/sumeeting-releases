Meeting notes for Google Meet, made on your Mac

SuMeeting records your Google Meet calls, transcribes them in Ukrainian and English, and turns the transcript into a summary with action points. Recordings and transcripts stay under your control — on your Mac or in your own Google Drive.

Download for macOS Chrome extension

What SuMeeting does

Records the call

The Chrome extension captures the audio (and video) of a Google Meet call together with your microphone. You start and stop recording yourself.

Transcribes with speaker labels

The macOS app transcribes the recording with the NVIDIA Canary model — locally on Apple Silicon, or on a cloud GPU if you choose — and labels who said what.

Summarizes

A summary and a list of action points are generated from the transcript with Claude Code, using your own Claude account.

Keeps your data yours

Transcripts and summaries live in a local database on your Mac. Recordings are stored locally or in a folder in your own Google Drive.

How SuMeeting uses your Google account

SuMeeting asks you to sign in with Google and requests two permissions. Both are optional to the extent described below, and you can revoke them at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

PermissionWhy SuMeeting needs it
Google Drive — files created by this app
drive.file		 Stores your meeting recordings in a SuMeeting folder in your Drive when you choose Drive as the recording storage, and plays them back from there. When the cloud transcription engine is selected, the recording is placed temporarily in SuMeeting/Transit so the transcription worker can fetch it; the file is deleted as soon as transcription finishes. SuMeeting can only see files it created — never your other Drive files.
Google Calendar — read-only access to events
calendar.events.readonly		 Finds the calendar event that matches the Google Meet call you recorded (by the Meet room code and start time) and reads its attendee list, so the meeting is saved with the correct participants instead of a best-effort list scraped from the Meet screen. SuMeeting never creates, edits or deletes calendar events.

Details of what is collected, stored and shared are in the Privacy Policy.

Requirements

  • macOS 13 or newer on Apple Silicon (M1 and later)
  • Google Chrome with the SuMeeting extension
  • A Google account (for sign-in, Drive storage and Calendar participants)
  • Claude Code (optional, for summaries and action points)

Install

  1. Download SuMeeting.dmg from the latest release and drag SuMeeting into Applications.
  2. Install the Chrome extension.
  3. Open SuMeeting, sign in with Google and follow the first-run setup.
